Section Assignment Guide

AI Automation Mode (Recommended)

When enabled, the system automatically processes all incoming enrollments without manual intervention.

  • Auto-Review: Validates all enrollment forms and required documents
  • Auto-Approve: Approves students who meet all requirements (complete forms + report card)
  • Auto-Assign: Places students in sections sequentially (fills Section 1 to capacity before Section 2)
  • Real-time: Processes new enrollments as they are submitted (24/7)

Manual Mode

When AI is disabled, you must manually review and approve each enrollment.

  • Manual Review: Go to each student's profile to review their documents
  • Manual Approval: Click "Approve" button on student edit page to approve enrollment
  • Manual Assignment: Drag students to sections or use dropdown menus on this page

Switching Modes

Use the toggle switch at the top of this page to enable or disable AI automation. Changes take effect immediately for new enrollments.
