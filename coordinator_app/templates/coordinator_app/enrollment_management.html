Enrollment Management Guide

AI Automation Mode (Recommended)

When enabled, the system automatically processes all incoming enrollments without manual intervention.

  • Auto-Review: Validates all enrollment forms and required documents
  • Auto-Approve: Approves students who meet all requirements
  • Auto-Assign: Places students in sections optimally

Manual Mode

When AI is disabled, you must manually review and approve each enrollment.

  • Manual Review: Review each student's documents
  • Manual Approval: Click "Approve" button to approve enrollment
  • Manual Assignment: Assign students to sections individually

Switching Modes

Use the toggle switch at the top of this page to enable or disable AI automation. Changes take effect immediately for new enrollments.
