School Policies and Terms

1. Enrollment Policy

Submission of this form does not guarantee automatic enrollment. All applications are subject to review based on academic standing, availability of slots in specific programs, and verification of submitted documents (Form 137, Birth Certificate, etc.).

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act, Zamboanga National High School West collects and processes personal and academic data solely for enrollment, sectioning, and academic record-keeping. Your data will be stored securely and will not be shared with third parties without consent.

Applicants must provide true and accurate grade information. Any discovered discrepancy between the manual entry and the official Report Card may result in the cancellation of the application or disciplinary action.
