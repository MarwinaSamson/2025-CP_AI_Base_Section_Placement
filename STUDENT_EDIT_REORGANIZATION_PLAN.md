# Student Edit Page - Complete Field Organization Plan

## Card Layout Structure (Following Screenshot):

### Row 1 (4 columns):

1. **Identity Information** (Basic Data)
   - Photo
   - LRN
   - Name (Last, First, Middle)
   - Age
   - Gender
   - Date of Birth
   - Place of Birth

2. **Contact & Background**
   - Address
   - Residence Barangay
   - Religion
   - Dialect Spoken
   - Ethnic Tribe

3. **Previous Education**
   - Last School Attended
   - Previous Grade & Section
   - Last School Year

4. **Student Status** (Spans 2 rows)
   - Enrolling As (New/Transferee/Returnee)
   - SPED Student? (Yes/No + Details)
   - Working Student? (Yes/No + Details)

### Row 2 (3 columns):

5. **Father's Information**
   - Family Name, First Name, Middle Name
   - Date of Birth / Age
   - Occupation
   - Address
   - Contact Number
   - Email

6. **Mother's Information**
   - Family Name, First Name, Middle Name
   - Date of Birth / Age
   - Occupation
   - Address
   - Contact Number
   - Email

7. **Parent/Guardian Photo**
   - Photo upload

### Row 3 (3 columns):

8. **Guardian Information** (with "Same as Mother" toggle)
   - Guardian Type (Father/Mother/Other)
   - Family Name, First Name, Middle Name
   - Date of Birth / Age
   - Occupation
   - Address
   - Relationship to Student
   - Contact Number
   - Email

### Row 4 (Full width split):

9. **Non-Academic Profile** (Spans 3 columns - Survey Data)
   All survey sections B-H with all checkboxes and radio options

10. **Academic Information** (Spans 1 column)
    - LRN
    - DOST Exam Result
    - Report Card Upload (Front)
    - Report Card Upload (Back)
    - Subject Grades (all 8 subjects)
    - Overall Average

### Row 5 (Full width):

11. **Document Requirements**
12. **Enrollment Placement** (Admin Only)
